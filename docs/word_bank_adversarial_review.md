# Built-In Word Bank Expansion Review

This pass expanded every built-in theme with a conservative adversarial review.
The goal was large growth without letting adjacent-theme drift pollute the banks.

## Review Rules

- Prefer concrete nouns over vibe words.
- Reject generic scenery, gear, or sentiment if it fits another bank better.
- Keep franchise-specific banks aligned to the franchise boundary.
- Fail closed on ambiguous additions instead of padding counts.
